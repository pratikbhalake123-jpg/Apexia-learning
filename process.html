The process

Clear steps.
Considered at every stage.

You’ll know what we’re working on, what comes next and where your feedback fits. Each stage builds on the last, with time to review along the way.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We discuss your learners, goals, material and context.
    You receive: learning objectives and project direction.

  2. 02

    Design

    We map the journey, plan the content and set a visual direction.
    You receive: a storyboard or content outline for review.

  3. 03

    Develop

    We build the module and shape the supporting learning content.
    You receive: a working draft to explore and review.

  4. 04

    Review

    We gather your feedback, refine the details and check clarity across devices.
    You receive: a polished version for final review.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    We prepare the completed work and organise the project handover.
    You receive: your final module and editable project files.
